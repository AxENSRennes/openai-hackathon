# Coffee Run

A miniature office sports broadcast. Six well-intentioned employees turn a coffee delivery into a physical disaster. The race, the cup, and the collision are the main visual content.

## Art direction

- Three-quarter orthographic camera, cutaway walls, and a raised diorama base.
- Rounded toy furniture, matte surfaces, warm wood, sage cabinets, cream tiles, and terracotta coffee equipment.
- Oversized heads, short legs, expressive falls, visible mugs, and persistent coffee puddles.
- Runner colors identify the same employee across their clothes, name tag, desk, arrival marker, and betting slip.
- Soft directional shadows ground objects. Steam, rolling chairs, spilled droplets, and victory confetti explain game events.
- The overview keeps the entire arena visible. Following a selected runner offers a closer view.

## Interface

Warm paper background and dark forest text. Condensed display type gives the broadcast a playful sports identity; the betting interface uses a readable sans serif. Chartreuse marks betting actions, orange marks coffee and incidents. Other colors belong to competitors.

The arena dominates the desktop screen. The lineup, live pool, and wager form sit alongside it. Phones open a focused betting slip without loading the 3D scene. Every pool percentage comes from actual stakes. The agent controller mode remains visible.

## Game rules

Take a cup from the shared kitchen, fill it at the shared machine, and deliver it to your own marked desk with at least 55% remaining. Agents choose pace, route, and aggression. Pushing, coffee theft, rolling chairs, and slippery puddles can change the result. Falls are recoverable. An expired race without delivery is a draw and refunds bets.

Betting closes before the race. Stakes use imaginary coins, are shared live, and pay out from the actual pool. Advice, audience sabotage, and voice are outside this version.
